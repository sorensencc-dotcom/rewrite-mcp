What is the time limit for filing a Claim? 

Florida has a two-year statute of limitations on personal injury cases. This means that you have two years from the date of your injury to file a claim or you may lose your right to recover. You can ensure that your claim is filed on time by contacting a personal injury attorney from The Fran Haasch Law Group as soon as possible after your accident.
