About Our Tampa Spa

Spa Evangeline is the ultimate Luxury Day Spa in South Tampa, FL. Nestled in the trendy Epicurean Hotel, both your skin and palate will be pampered with flavors of relaxation. We’re here to serve our guests, as well as the entire Bay Area.

Sometimes you just have to escape. And the best way to escape is by visiting Spa Evangeline to refresh, relax, and to regain your sense of peace. We offer a luxury spa experience, with decadent services that are meant to pamper your body, mind, and soul.
