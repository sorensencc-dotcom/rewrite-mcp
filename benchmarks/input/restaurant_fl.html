Welcome to MadFish St. Pete Beach

Step into MadFish St. Pete Beach, where coastal charm meets warm, neighborhood hospitality. Founded by local Pinellas native Mark Tomlinson, MadFish was created as a place for friends, family, and neighbors to gather and enjoy fresh, thoughtfully prepared cuisine in a relaxed, inviting atmosphere. With a focus on quality, locally sourced ingredients and a passion for memorable dining experiences, every visit promises something special from the moment you walk through the door to the last bite.

Savor the Taste, Delight in the Flavor

At MadFish St. Pete Beach, every dish is a celebration of bold, fresh flavors. From locally sourced ingredients to carefully crafted recipes, our goal is simple: to deliver taste experiences that linger long after the last bite. Each plate is thoughtfully prepared with a balance of freshness, seasoning, and creativity a true reflection of coastal culinary artistry.
